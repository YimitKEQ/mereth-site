# Brag Plan: Mereth Roleplay

## What is this app?
merethroleplay.com is the official handbook for Mereth, a serious-roleplay Skyrim multiplayer
server on SkyMP set in 4E 185, where the jarl, the guard and the blacksmith are all players
rather than NPCs, and the site documents every system the server runs.

## The angle
Every Skyrim trailer ever made promises you a hero. This one promises you other people.

The strongest thing about Mereth is not a feature list, it is an absence: no global chat, no
quest markers, no NPC filling the chairs that matter. The site already says this better than
any marketing line could, in the hero paragraph: "The jarl who grants your rank, the guard who
arrests you, the smith who makes your sword." That triad is the video. Three roles, three real
people, stated flatly over real footage of the province.

The restraint is the creative direction. The site's own design law is "cold chrome, warm only
on the action", ease-out only, nothing bounces, "this world is heavy". A video that bounces,
flashes or swells would contradict the product it is selling. Every claim in it is quoted from
the live site, because the site is careful about not claiming what is not shipped.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Near-black. Wind. Two lines of the site's own negative space, one after the other, in the
handbook's display face:

NO GLOBAL CHAT.
NO QUEST MARKERS.

An absence is a stronger hook than a promise, and it is the exact thing that filters the
audience: anybody who wants a quest marker has already stopped watching, which is correct.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The triad, one line at a time, each over real in-game footage: THE JARL WHO GRANTS YOUR RANK
  / THE GUARD WHO ARRESTS YOU / THE SMITH WHO MAKES YOUR SWORD. Each with a low impact under
  it. The rhythm is a hammer, not a montage.
- The turn: "ALL OF THEM ARE PLAYERS." This is the only sentence in the video that explains
  anything, and it earns its place by being the point.
- The handbook itself: a real frame of merethroleplay.com, pushed in slowly, so the video shows
  the thing that was actually built and not only the world it documents. Under it, the true
  scale: 51 skills documented, 457 spells indexed, 250 releases tracked.

## Outro / punchline
The site's own closing headline, which is already the best line on the page:

BRING A CHARACTER WHO WANTS SOMETHING.

Then the wordmark, the eyebrow it wears on the live site ("Serious roleplay, 4E 185"), and the
domain. No call to action beyond the address, because the site is the call to action.

## User flow worth showing
Entry is the handbook, not the game: a stranger lands on merethroleplay.com, reads what the
place is, and joins the Discord. The video shows the real hero frame and the real live counts
(4,315 in the Discord, 2,425 online, 201 souls abroad) as its one product beat. The rest is the
world those systems describe, because the product's whole argument is about the world.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: a provincial record, not a product launch. Skyrim trailer scale with the
  site's restraint: heavy, cold, slow, and every word quoted from the live page.
- Interpretation: long holds, full-bleed footage, large tracked caps in Friz Quadrata, ease-out
  only, no bounce, no glow, no particles. Motion is a slow push, never a swing.

## Format: landscape - 1920x1080
## Duration: 21 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#10161a` (Page, cold near-black slate, never `#000`)
- Surface: `#1b2429` (Stone), rules `#2f3d47` (Iron)
- Accent: `#9fb8c4` (Frost) for ornament and headings
- Action: `#b89258` (Brass), the only warm object, reserved for the final address
- Text: `#e8e4d9` (Bone, never `#fff`)
- Display font: Friz Quadrata Std (local OTFs in `src/fonts/`, the site's real face)
- Body font: Exo
- Strongest visual element: the Nordic interlace corner bracket, drawn in markup, plus the real
  4K province footage in `public/art/`

## Share copy (draft)
Mereth is a Skyrim roleplay server where the jarl, the guard and the smith are all players. We
wrote the handbook for it: merethroleplay.com

## Audio direction
- Role: designed near-silence. Cold wind bed with sparse, heavy accents.
- Music: none, deliberately. The bundled library is upbeat corporate ("Happy Beats / Business
  Moves") and would contradict every visual decision in the piece. An epic stock trailer bed
  would be worse: it is the sound of the generic fantasy ad this server is not.
- Music treatment: not applicable. The bed is a synthesised wind layer built locally from
  filtered noise, swelling under the triad and falling away under the wordmark.
- Music cue guidance: none. Timing follows the reading pace of the type, which is the correct
  master for a piece with this little on screen.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none. Nothing on screen should pulse.
- SFX posture: sparse. Snow footsteps in the opening, one low impact per triad line, one
  heavier impact on the wordmark. Kenney CC0 pack, bundled with the skill.
- Audio-coupled moments: each triad line lands with its impact; the wordmark lands with the
  heaviest; the wind fades last.
- Restraint rule: no whoosh, no riser, no cymbal, no sub-drop. If it would sound at home in a
  mobile game advert it does not go in.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 - No global chat - 4.0s
Full-bleed province footage, graded down hard so the type carries. "NO GLOBAL CHAT." settles,
holds, then "NO QUEST MARKERS." arrives beneath it. Both in tracked Friz caps, Bone on near
black.
Sequential/interaction: yes, two lines arriving one after the other, roughly 1.4s apart.
Audio intent: cold and empty. Wind alone, with two snow footsteps under the line entrances.
Audio-coupled idea: footstep on each line settle.
Music: none.
Transition mood: slow crossfade -> Scene 2

### Scene 2 - The triad - 7.0s
Three beats over three different frames of the province. One line each, large, centred, each
replacing the last with a slow push-in rather than a cut:
THE JARL WHO GRANTS YOUR RANK / THE GUARD WHO ARRESTS YOU / THE SMITH WHO MAKES YOUR SWORD.
Sequential/interaction: yes, three reveals at roughly 2.3s each.
Audio intent: a hammer falling three times. The wind rises slightly across the three.
Audio-coupled idea: one low impact per line.
Music: none.
Transition mood: hard -> Scene 3

### Scene 3 - All of them are players - 3.0s
The three lines collapse into one statement, held still and long: "ALL OF THEM ARE PLAYERS."
Frost accent rule above and below, the site's interlace bracket at the corners.
Sequential/interaction: none. Stillness is the point.
Audio intent: the wind alone again, and the absence of an impact where the viewer expects one.
Audio-coupled idea: none, deliberately.
Music: none.
Transition mood: clean -> Scene 4

### Scene 4 - The handbook - 3.5s
The real merethroleplay.com hero frame, held inside the site's own interlace frame, pushed in
slowly from 1.00 to 1.04. Three counted figures rise beneath it in tabular numerals:
51 SKILLS DOCUMENTED / 457 SPELLS INDEXED / 250 RELEASES TRACKED.
Sequential/interaction: yes, the three figures arrive left to right, 0.25s apart.
Audio intent: quieter, indoors, the sound of a record rather than a landscape.
Audio-coupled idea: a soft interface tick per figure.
Music: none.
Transition mood: soft -> Scene 5

### Scene 5 - Bring a character - 3.5s
Near-black. "BRING A CHARACTER WHO WANTS SOMETHING." holds alone, then falls away to the
wordmark: eyebrow SERIOUS ROLEPLAY, 4E 185 above MERETH in the display face, with
merethroleplay.com beneath it in Brass, the only warm thing in the whole video.
Sequential/interaction: yes, line first, then wordmark.
Audio intent: the heaviest impact of the piece under the wordmark, then the wind alone, fading
to nothing over the address.
Audio-coupled idea: impact on the wordmark settle.
Music: none.
Transition mood: final.

**Music mood for this video:** none, intentionally silent of music; a designed wind and impact layer carries it.
**Audio summary:** Wind from the first frame to the last, three hammer falls under the triad, a
deliberate silence where the fourth would be, and one heavy landing under the wordmark.

**Total: 4.0 + 7.0 + 3.0 + 3.5 + 3.5 = 21.0s**
