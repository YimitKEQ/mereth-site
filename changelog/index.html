  4. 0.71.02026-09-0333 notes

    This is an important update, we been struggling with voice issues for awhile, after several optimizations and moving it to its own separate connection and thread from the game world, in theory we should hold strong even if the rest of the world starts failing, voice should now be the last to go!

    Another big feature, well rested buff has been increased to 100% that is 2x xp for the first hour, this is to help the casual player keep up and help alleviate the lower levels and grind! You only get 3 well rested buffs, they diminish in potency before a 16 hour cooldown.

    Last but not least, long awaited faction stones are done and welcome a new college of winterhold!!! with working clocks and other great features!

    Outside of that, more functions in dungeons are working, new loading system that increases load times, makes it more immersive, and everything feels really smooth and performant on this update!

    I hope you all enjoy this, we been working all day on this!
