The holdstone is the spine of the server. You pledge to a hold, hold a rank in it, and the rank carries real mechanical benefit rather than a title alone: a blacksmith rank carries smithing, guards carry light armour, a court mage carries spell points. Professions are ranks inside this system, not a separate one.

Parcels are the property system. A hold divides into named parcels, and a parcel is the closest thing Mereth has to owning something. Jarls and stewards grant them to citizens. Doors and containers bind to a parcel as fixtures, and holders carry parcel keys.

  • Anyone
  • All citizens
  • Rank and above
  • Named residents
  • Invite only
  • Unlocked

A parcel can also demand a minimum rank, and access can be granted to people outside the hold entirely, so a workshop, a shop back room or a stash can be shared across hold lines.

A bound container stops regenerating

The single most useful storage fact on the server. An ordinary container regenerates its contents, which means it is not safe to keep anything in. Bind it to a parcel through the holdstone and it stops.
